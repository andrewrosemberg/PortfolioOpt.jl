Uncertainty: distributions and ambiguity sets

The returns of the next period are described by an AmbiguitySet: either a distribution (any multivariate Distributions.Sampleable, e.g. MvNormal, a MixtureModel or DeterministicSamples), or a CenteredAmbiguitySet around a nominal distribution (BudgetSet, EllipticalSet, MomentUncertainty, DuWassersteinBall). A forecaster (AbstractForecaster, e.g. EmpiricalGaussian, EmpiricalSamples) builds one from past returns with forecast.

Measures, terms and formulations

A PortfolioRiskMeasure wraps an ambiguity set: ExpectedReturn(d) is the mean return for a distribution and the worst case mean for an ambiguity set. Measures, and other AbstractPortfolioTerms such as ConeRegularizer, are used as weighted ObjectiveTerms and as RiskConstraints of a PortfolioFormulation. Which measures are implemented for which sets, and the reformulation of each, is on the Theory pages.

A formulation is data: it does not depend on a model or a solver. portfolio_model! adds it to a JuMP model, calling calculate_measure! for each term. With record_measures=true it returns, for the uid of each term, the JuMP expression of its value, which can be read after solving (value(pointers[uid])).

Markets

A PortfolioMarket defines the feasible portfolios (market_model), holds the budget and the current decision (bids), and computes the profit once the realized returns are known (clear_market!, total_profit). VolumeMarket is the market of the package: long only or with short selling, a fee per unit invested and a risk free rate for the money not invested.

The functions that connect formulations and markets are:

FunctionDoes
build_portfolio_model(market, formulation, optimizer)market_model + portfolio_model!, not solved
change_bids!(market, model, w)solves and stores value.(w) as the bids
change_bids!(market, formulation, optimizer)both of the above
portfolio_weights(formulation, N, optimizer)the bids on a fresh VolumeMarket

Backtests

sequential_backtest_market walks forward over a MarketHistory (e.g. a VolumeMarketHistory of a TimeArray of returns). For each date it calls the strategy with the market and the returns strictly before that date (past_prices), clears the market with the returns of the date, records the state, and reinvests the profit. The StateRecorders :wealth, :returns and :decisions are recorded by default (get_records); if the strategy returns the pointers of change_bids! (with record_measures=true) and an optimization_recorder is passed, the value of every term is recorded too. See the Model Assessment example.

Optional integrations

The core depends only on JuMP, MathOptInterface, Distributions, PDMats, ProgressMeter, Reexport and standard libraries. These integrations are package extensions, loaded when the other package is:

PackageAdds
TimeSeries.jla TimeArray of returns as the history of a VolumeMarketHistory
MarketData.jlTestUtils.get_test_data() (and loads TimeSeries)
OptimalBids.jlOptimalBids.change_bids!, clear_market! and calculate_profit accept any PortfolioMarket

The submodule PortfolioOpt.TestUtils has get_test_data (prices of 6 assets, needs using MarketData), mean_variance and max_sharpe.
