Guarantees

Each mathematical claim of the Theory pages has a name. The test with the same name, in test/guarantees.jl, checks it numerically on random instances (fixed seeds) with the solver tolerance 1e-5. They are checks, not proofs, but a proof of a claim can refer to its name.

Unless stated otherwise the claims hold under the assumptions of the measure (see its page) and the direction assumption.

NameClaimPage
cvar_ru_lower_bound$z - \mathbb{E}[(z - R)^+]/\alpha \leq -\mathrm{CVaR}_\alpha(R)$ for every $z$Risk measures
cvar_ru_exactthe linear reformulation of ConditionalExpectedReturn(s; α) equals the sorted sample tail mean, for $\alpha S$ integer or not, and $\alpha = 1$ gives the meanRisk measures
worst_case_return_exactWorstCaseReturn(s) and ConditionalExpectedReturn(s; α=0) equal the smallest sample returnRisk measures
variance_epigraph_exactthe rotated SOC epigraph of Variance and the SOC epigraph of SqrtVariance equal $w^\top \Sigma w$ and its square root when minimizedRisk measures
risk_constraint_feasiblethe optimal portfolio satisfies SqrtVariance, Variance, ConditionalExpectedReturn and CertaintyEquivalent constraints evaluated directly (not through the reformulation)The portfolio problem
cone_regularizer_exacta penalized ConeRegularizer equals the norm of the optimal portfolioRisk measures
certainty_equivalent_le_mean$CE(R) \leq \mathbb{E}[R]$ and $\mathbb{E}[u(R)] \leq u(\mathbb{E}[R])$ (Jensen) for Gaussian and sampled returnsRisk measures
elliptical_closed_formthe worst case expected return over EllipticalSet is $\hat{r}^\top w - \delta \lVert \Sigma^{1/2} w \rVert_2$Ambiguity sets
budget_set_strong_dualitythe dual expression of BudgetSet equals the primal worst case LP and the greedy closed form, also with short positionsAmbiguity sets
moment_worst_case_le_nominalworst case expected return over MomentUncertainty $\leq$ nominal mean return, for every portfolio and at the optimumAmbiguity sets
moment_monotone_in_radiusthe worst case conditional expected return is nonincreasing in $\gamma_1$ and $\gamma_2$Ambiguity sets
moment_cvar_le_member_cvarworst case conditional expected return $\leq$ that of the Gaussian with the nominal momentsAmbiguity sets
moment_bounded_support_betweenunbounded support value $\leq$ box support value $\leq$ nominal mean returnAmbiguity sets

wasserstein_monotone_in_radiusthe worst case expected return over DuWassersteinBall is nonincreasing in $\epsilon$ and below the sample mean, for $p = 1, 2, \infty$Ambiguity sets

The closed forms of the moment set ($\hat{r}^\top w - \sqrt{\gamma_1} \sigma$ and $\hat{r}^\top w - \sqrt{\gamma_2 (1 - \alpha)/\alpha} \, \sigma$) and of the exponential utility (Gaussian, Gaussian mixture and samples) are checked in test/dro_mean.jl and test/exponential_utility.jl.

Adding a guarantee

A new measure or ambiguity set should come with the claims its reformulation relies on (a closed form, a bound, a monotonicity), stated in its docstring under "Guarantees" and checked by a named testset in test/guarantees.jl.
